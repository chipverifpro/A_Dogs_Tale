Rotating Lawn Sprinkler Set - Unity Notes

Files:
- rotating_lawn_sprinkler_with_water_arc.obj
- rotating_lawn_sprinkler_no_water.obj
- rotating_lawn_sprinkler_set.mtl

Design:
- Two matching sprinkler state models:
  1. Rotating lawn sprinkler with visible arcing water spray.
  2. Same sprinkler without water for idle/off state.
- Green sled-style sprinkler base.
- Rear hose connector and hose stub.
- Central rotating riser/head.
- Front spray nozzle, impact flapper, arc adjustment pins.
- Water version includes multiple arcing streams, droplets, ground splash, and wet hazard guide area.

Unity setup:
1. Import both OBJ files and the shared MTL together.
2. Create prefabs under something like:
   Assets/A_Dogs_Tale/Resources/Prefabs/Props/Garden/
3. Delete or hide Guide_* groups before final visual use, or keep them on an editor/helper layer.
4. Use the dry model for Off/Idle.
5. Use the water model for On/Active, or keep both as children and toggle visibility.
6. Unity may import water materials as opaque. If so:
   - Set water materials to Transparent.
   - Use alpha around 0.35-0.55.
   - Increase Smoothness/Specular.
7. If animating later:
   - Rotate the sprinkler head around Guide_Rotation_Pivot_Point.
   - Spawn water particles from Guide_Water_Emission_Point.
   - Use the OBJ water arc as a cheap static visual placeholder.

Suggested colliders:
- Small BoxCollider for base.
- Trigger over Guide_Wet_Hazard_Arc_Area_delete_or_hide while active.
- Optional trigger at Guide_Interaction_Point_TurnSprinklerOnOff.

Gameplay ideas for A Dog's Tale:
- Dogs may avoid, chase, drink from, or play in the sprinkler.
- Creates splash/noise events.
- Can make wet pawprints or dilute scent trails.
- Can scare cats/squirrels or flush frogs/birds from hiding.
- Puzzle use: turn sprinkler on to block a path, clean mud, reveal wet tracks, or distract a Roomba.

OBJ limitation:
- OBJ stores mesh/materials only.
- It does not store Unity colliders, triggers, particles, water shaders, scripts, rotation animation, sound, or wet/scent behavior.

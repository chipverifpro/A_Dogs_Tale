Desk Service Bell / Bellhop Tap Bell - Unity Notes

Files:
- desk_service_bell.obj
- desk_service_bell.mtl

Design:
- Small hotel/service desk bell.
- Round black rubber foot/base shadow.
- Brass/gold plinth and shiny dome.
- Push/tap button on top.
- Visible internal clapper ball and arm.
- Small screw heads, reflection streaks, and optional yellow ringing arcs.
- Guide markers included for interaction, collider, button press, and noise source.

Important named groups:
- Round_black_rubber_foot_shadow
- Round_bell_base_plinth
- Thin_bright_base_rim
- Main_shiny_service_bell_dome
- Bell_dome_rolled_lower_lip
- Bell_dome_dark_open_under_shadow
- Central_button_stem
- Round_tap_button_on_top
- Rounded_top_button_cap
- Visible_internal_clapper_ball
- Visible_internal_clapper_arm
- Optional_ring_sound_arc_*
- Guide_Collider_Bell_Box_delete_or_hide
- Guide_Button_Press_Point
- Guide_Interaction_Point_RingBell
- Guide_Noise_Source_Ring_Event_delete_or_hide

Unity setup:
1. Import OBJ and MTL together.
2. Create a prefab under something like:
   Assets/A_Dogs_Tale/Resources/Prefabs/Props/Interactables/
3. Delete or hide Guide_* groups before final visual use, or keep them on an editor/helper layer.
4. Suggested colliders:
   - Small BoxCollider or CapsuleCollider around bell body.
   - Small trigger/collider at Guide_Button_Press_Point.
5. If you keep Optional_ring_sound_arc_* as a visual, enable it briefly when the bell rings.
   Otherwise hide/delete those groups.

Gameplay ideas:
- Dog can tap it with paw or nose.
- Emits a sharp Noise event when rung.
- Could summon a human/bellhop/servant/NPC.
- Can be used as a puzzle object: ring bell to open door, call help, attract attention, or distract a Roomba/cat.
- Good command/action target: RingBell, PressButton, MakeNoise, Summon.

OBJ limitation:
- OBJ stores mesh/materials only.
- It does not store Unity colliders, triggers, scripts, sound effects, button animation, ringing particles, or summon behavior.

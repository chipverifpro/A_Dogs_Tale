Outdoor Water Tap Set - Unity Notes

Files:
- outdoor_water_tap_with_pouring_water.obj
- outdoor_water_tap_no_water.obj
- outdoor_water_tap_set.mtl

Design:
- Two matching outdoor tap / faucet state models:
  1. Tap with water pouring out.
  2. Same tap without water for off/idle state.
- Includes:
  - Wall mounting plate
  - Vertical supply pipe
  - Brass faucet body
  - Red turn handle
  - Downturned outlet nozzle
  - Threaded hose connection
  - Side hose connection stub with short attached hose end
  - Patina/rust/wear details
  - Guide markers for interaction, hose connection, water emission, and puddle area

Unity setup:
1. Import both OBJ files and outdoor_water_tap_set.mtl together.
2. Create prefabs under something like:
   Assets/A_Dogs_Tale/Resources/Prefabs/Props/Garden/
3. Delete or hide Guide_* groups before final visual use, or keep them on an editor/helper layer.
4. Use outdoor_water_tap_no_water.obj for Off.
5. Use outdoor_water_tap_with_pouring_water.obj for On, or put both under one prefab and toggle visibility.
6. Unity may import water as opaque. If so:
   - Set water materials to Transparent.
   - Use alpha around 0.35-0.55.
   - Increase Smoothness/Specular.
7. For animation:
   - Rotate Round_turn_handle_wheel / crossbars when toggled.
   - Spawn particles from Guide_Water_Emission_Point.
   - Use the OBJ water stream as a cheap placeholder visual.

Suggested colliders:
- BoxCollider around tap assembly.
- Small trigger at Guide_Interaction_Point_TurnHandle.
- Optional trigger at Guide_Hose_Connection_Point.
- Wet hazard/scent-dilution trigger over Guide_Wet_Puddle_Area_delete_or_hide when on.

Gameplay ideas for A Dog's Tale:
- Dog can sniff, drink from, paw/nose the handle, or react to water noise.
- Tap can fill a bowl, create puddles, wet pawprints, or dilute scent trails.
- Hose connection can feed the sprinkler model.
- Puzzle use: turn tap on to start sprinkler, reveal wet tracks, distract Roomba, or make frogs/birds appear.

OBJ limitation:
- OBJ stores mesh/materials only.
- It does not store Unity colliders, triggers, scripts, particles, water shaders, handle animation, sound, hose simulation, or water gameplay logic.

Laundry Basket Full of Dirty Laundry - Unity Notes

Files:
- laundry_basket_dirty_laundry.obj
- laundry_basket_dirty_laundry.mtl

Design:
- Slotted plastic laundry basket with a blue rim and white side bars.
- Piled dirty laundry visible above the basket.
- Includes shirts, towels, socks, washcloth, dropped floor laundry, and optional scent wisps.
- Good scent-heavy prop for dog investigation gameplay.

Important named groups:
- Large basket structure:
  - Basket_top_*_rounded_rim
  - Basket_bottom_*_rail
  - Basket_*_vertical_slotted_bar_*
  - Basket_*_horizontal_slot_rail_*
  - Basket_bottom_grid_*_rail_*
- Laundry:

  - Laundry_pile_*_lump_*

Unity setup:
1. Import OBJ and MTL together.
2. Create a prefab under something like:
   Assets/A_Dogs_Tale/Resources/Prefabs/Props/Laundry/
3. Delete or hide Guide_* groups before final visual use, or keep them on an editor/helper layer.
4. Suggested colliders:
   - BoxCollider for the basket body.
   - Optional trigger over Guide_Scent_Source_DirtyLaundry_delete_or_hide.
   - Optional pickup collider for Dropped_sock_on_floor_left.
5. The basket is visually slotted, but not physically hollow unless you build custom colliders.
   For gameplay, use simple colliders unless dogs need to climb inside.

Gameplay ideas:
- Strong human/household scent source.
- Dogs can sniff, dig through, steal a sock, carry laundry, or track owner scent.
- Roomba can bump the dropped laundry.
- A hidden clue/toy/treat can be placed inside the laundry pile.
- Good target for ReactToScent and ReactToScentSourceFound.

OBJ limitation:
- OBJ stores mesh/materials only.
- It does not store Unity colliders, triggers, scripts, scent emitters, cloth physics, or pickup behavior.

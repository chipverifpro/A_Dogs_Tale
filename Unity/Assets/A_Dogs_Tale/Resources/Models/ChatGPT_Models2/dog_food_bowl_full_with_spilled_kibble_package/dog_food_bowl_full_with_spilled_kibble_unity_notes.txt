Dog Food Bowl Full of Kibble with Spilled Pieces - Unity Notes

Files:
- dog_food_bowl_full_with_spilled_kibble.obj
- dog_food_bowl_full_with_spilled_kibble.mtl

Design:
- Red plastic dog food bowl.
- Open bowl shell with darker inner wall.
- Mounded kibble surface inside.
- Individual kibble pieces inside the bowl.
- Larger spilled kibble pieces and tiny crumbs in front of the bowl.
- Small bone-shaped label on the front.
- Guide markers for collider, scent, interaction, and spilled-food target placement.

Important named groups:
- Dog_food_bowl_outer_red_plastic_shell
- Dog_food_bowl_inner_dark_red_wall
- Dog_food_bowl_thick_rounded_top_rim
- Dog_food_bowl_inner_bottom_disk

- Guide_Scent_Source_Food_delete_or_hide

- Guide_Spilled_Kibble_Target

Unity setup:
1. Import OBJ and MTL together.
2. Create a prefab under something like:
   Assets/A_Dogs_Tale/Resources/Prefabs/Props/Dog/
3. Delete or hide Guide_* groups before final visual use, or keep them on an editor/helper layer.
4. Suggested colliders:
   - Cylinder-like collider or short CapsuleCollider for the bowl.
   - Optional trigger over Guide_Scent_Source_Food_delete_or_hide.
   - Optional individual colliders only for large spilled kibble if dogs can pick/eat them.
5. The guide and shadow materials include MTL transparency, but Unity may import them as opaque.
   If so, either hide/delete those meshes or set material Surface Type to Transparent.

Gameplay ideas:
- Strong food scent source.
- Dog can sniff, eat, guard, spill more food, or follow a kibble trail.
- Roomba can scatter the spilled pieces.
- Squirrels/birds can steal kibble.
- Useful for hunger, reward, training, or forbidden-food behavior.
- ReactToScent and ReactToScentSourceFound can trigger near the bowl.

OBJ limitation:
- OBJ stores mesh/materials only.
- It does not store Unity colliders, triggers, scripts, scent emitters, pickup/eating behavior, or particle effects.

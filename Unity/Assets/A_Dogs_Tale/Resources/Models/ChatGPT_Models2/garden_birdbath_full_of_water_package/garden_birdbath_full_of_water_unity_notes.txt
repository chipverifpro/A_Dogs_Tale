Garden Birdbath Full of Water - Unity Notes

Files:
- garden_birdbath_full_of_water.obj
- garden_birdbath_full_of_water.mtl

Design:
- Weathered stone garden birdbath.
- Round base, flared pedestal foot, carved center column, shallow stone bowl.
- Filled with a transparent rippled water surface.
- Includes ripple rings, rim droplets, wet stone spots, and moss patches.
- Guide markers included for bird landing, water trigger, dog sniff/drink interaction, and colliders.

Unity setup:
1. Import OBJ and MTL together.
2. Create a prefab under something like:
   Assets/A_Dogs_Tale/Resources/Prefabs/Props/Garden/
3. Delete or hide Guide_* groups before final visual use, or keep them on an editor/helper layer.
4. Suggested colliders:
   - CapsuleCollider or cylinder-like collider for pedestal.
   - Shallow BoxCollider or MeshCollider for basin/rim if characters can touch it.
   - Trigger over Guide_Water_Source_Trigger_delete_or_hide.
5. Unity may import water materials as opaque. If so:
   - Set water materials to Transparent.
   - Use alpha around 0.35-0.55.
   - Increase Smoothness/Specular.

Gameplay ideas:
- Bird landing point / attractor.
- Dog can sniff or drink from it if tall enough.
- Squirrels/birds can perch on rim.
- Roomba can bump pedestal but probably cannot reach water.
- Can generate water scent, wet pawprints, splash noise, or bird activity.

OBJ limitation:
- OBJ stores mesh/materials only.
- It does not store Unity colliders, triggers, scripts, water shader settings, bird AI, or splash effects.

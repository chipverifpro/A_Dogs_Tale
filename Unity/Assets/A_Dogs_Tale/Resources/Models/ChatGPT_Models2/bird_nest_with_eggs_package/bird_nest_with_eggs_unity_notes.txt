Bird Nest with Eggs OBJ - Unity Notes

Files:
- bird_nest_with_eggs.obj
- bird_nest_with_eggs.mtl

Design:
- Small woven bird nest prop.
- Layered twig rings, loose cross twigs, dry grass lining, and a dark inner cup shadow.
- Three pale blue speckled eggs.
- Loose feathers, moss/leaf bits, and guide markers.
- Good for garden/tree/birdbath scenes.

Important named groups:
- Soft_shadow_under_nest
- Soft_dry_grass_inner_lining
- Concave_inner_nest_dark_cup_shadow
- Woven_outer_twig_ring_*_segment_*
- Loose_cross_twig_*
- Dry_grass_lining_strand_*
- Egg_front_left
- Egg_front_right
- Egg_back_center
- Egg_*_tiny_speckle_*
- Loose_white_feather_on_left_rim
- Loose_grey_feather_near_front
- Small_down_feather_inside_cup
- Small_leaf_or_moss_patch_*
- Guide_Collider_Nest_Box_delete_or_hide
- Guide_Bird_Landing_Point
- Guide_Egg_Interest_Point
- Guide_Dog_Sniff_Point
- Guide_Scent_Source_Nest_Area_delete_or_hide

Unity setup:
1. Import OBJ and MTL together.
2. Create a prefab under something like:
   Assets/A_Dogs_Tale/Resources/Prefabs/Props/Nature/
3. Delete or hide Guide_* groups before final visual use, or keep them on an editor/helper layer.
4. Suggested colliders:
   - Small BoxCollider or SphereCollider around the nest.
   - Optional trigger around Guide_Scent_Source_Nest_Area_delete_or_hide.
   - Optional small trigger at Guide_Bird_Landing_Point for bird AI.
5. If this will sit in a tree, make it a child of a branch/perch transform.

Gameplay ideas for A Dog's Tale:
- Bird landing/perch/rest target.
- Dog can sniff it, but perhaps should not disturb it.
- Squirrels/cats may be interested in eggs.
- Birds can react defensively if a dog gets too close.
- Can be a scent/vision clue in garden exploration.
- Good behaviors:
  - ReactToBirdNest
  - SniffNest
  - GuardNest
  - BirdReturnToNest
  - BirdAlarmCall

OBJ limitation:
- OBJ stores mesh/materials only.
- It does not store Unity colliders, triggers, scripts, bird AI, egg pickup logic, nest parenting, or animation.

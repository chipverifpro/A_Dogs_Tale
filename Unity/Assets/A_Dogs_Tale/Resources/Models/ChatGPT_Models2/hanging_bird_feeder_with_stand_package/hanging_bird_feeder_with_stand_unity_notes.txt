Hanging Bird Feeder with Stand - Unity Notes

Files:
- hanging_bird_feeder_with_stand.obj
- hanging_bird_feeder_with_stand.mtl

Design:
- Freestanding garden shepherd-hook stand.
- Curved metal hook with hanging chain.
- Hanging bird feeder with green roof, clear seed tube, seed column, seed tray, feeding ports, and perch rods.
- Includes loose seed in tray and spilled seed on the ground.
- Guide markers for bird landing, feeding, scent/seed area, hanging pivot, colliders, and dog sniff point.

Unity setup:
1. Import OBJ and MTL together.
2. Create a prefab under something like:
   Assets/A_Dogs_Tale/Resources/Prefabs/Props/Garden/
3. Delete or hide Guide_* groups before final visual use, or keep them on an editor/helper layer.
4. Unity may import clear plastic as opaque. If so:
   - Set clear_plastic and clear_plastic_highlight materials to Transparent.
   - Use alpha around 0.30-0.55.
   - Increase Smoothness/Specular.
5. Suggested colliders:
   - Thin CapsuleCollider for stand pole.
   - Small BoxCollider/SphereCollider around feeder body.
   - Trigger near Guide_Bird_Landing_Perch_Point and Guide_Bird_Feeding_Point.
   - Trigger around Guide_Scent_Source_Seed_Area_delete_or_hide for dog sniff/spilled seed interactions.
6. If you want the feeder to sway:
   - Parent the feeder and chain under a child object pivoting near Guide_Hanging_Pivot_Point.
   - Add a simple pendulum/spring animation in Unity.

Gameplay ideas for A Dog's Tale:
- Birds land, feed, then flee when dog approaches.
- Dogs sniff spilled seed, chase birds, or knock the stand.
- Squirrels can climb/raid it.
- Roomba can bump the stand and spill seed.
- Can pair with seagull, duck, squirrel, bird nest, birdbath, and sprinkler props.
- Useful behaviors:
  - BirdLandAtFeeder
  - BirdFeed
  - BirdFleeFromDog
  - DogSniffSeed
  - SquirrelRaidFeeder
  - ReactToBirdMovement

OBJ limitation:
- OBJ stores mesh/materials only.
- It does not store Unity colliders, triggers, scripts, bird AI, feeder swing physics, seed particle effects, or behavior state.

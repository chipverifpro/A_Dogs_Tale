Red Fire Hydrant OBJ - Unity Notes

Files:
- red_fire_hydrant.obj
- red_fire_hydrant.mtl

Design:
- Bright red fire hydrant prop.
- Wide base flange, main barrel, upper bonnet, domed top, top nut.
- Two side nozzles and one large front pumper nozzle.
- Bolts, dark gasket rings, chipped paint, small rust/water stain, and optional scent wisps.
- Guide markers included for collider, sniff interaction, and scent-mark point.

Important named groups:
- Wide_round_base_flange_red
- Base_black_gasket_ring
- Lower_tapered_hydrant_body
- Main_cylindrical_hydrant_barrel

- Guide_Scent_Mark_Point
- Guide_Scent_Source_Area_delete_or_hide

Unity setup:
1. Import OBJ and MTL together.
2. Create a prefab under something like:
   Assets/A_Dogs_Tale/Resources/Prefabs/Props/Street/
3. Delete or hide Guide_* groups before final visual use, or keep them on an editor/helper layer.
4. Suggested colliders:
   - CapsuleCollider around main body.
   - Optional BoxCollider or SphereCollider for side nozzles if dogs bump into them.
   - Trigger near Guide_Interaction_Point_SniffHydrant.
5. Optional_scent_wisp_* can be hidden if your scent visualization system handles scent separately.

Gameplay ideas:
- Classic dog sniff / scent-mark object.
- Strong scent landmark for navigation.
- Can act as a neighborhood waypoint.
- Dogs can investigate, mark, follow previous scent, or react to another dog's scent.
- Could be a puzzle clue: hydrant scent points toward route, gate, or lost dog.

OBJ limitation:
- OBJ stores mesh/materials only.
- It does not store Unity colliders, triggers, scripts, scent emitters, particle effects, or interaction logic.

Modular Picket Fence Panel Low Wall - Unity Notes

Files:
- modular_picket_fence_panel_low_wall.obj
- modular_picket_fence_panel_low_wall.mtl

Design:
- A reusable low wall / picket fence panel.
- Traditional white painted wood style.
- Two square end posts, horizontal rear rails, nine pointed pickets.
- Slight variation in picket heights and subtle paint chips/wood grain.
- Designed as one modular segment that can be repeated to form a fence line.

Approximate dimensions:
- Length: 3.0 Unity units
- Height: about 1.1 to 1.25 Unity units including post caps
- Depth/thickness: about 0.12 Unity units visually

Important named groups:
- Left_square_fence_post
- Right_square_fence_post
- Left_post_rounded_cap
- Right_post_rounded_cap
- Back_upper_horizontal_support_rail
- Back_lower_horizontal_support_rail
- Back_bottom_ground_kick_board
- Picket_*_pointed_board_rect_board
- Picket_*_pointed_board_pointed_cap
- Picket_*_subtle_vertical_woodgrain
- Small_paint_chip_*
- Small_dark_nail_head_*
- Guide_Snap_Left_End_delete_or_hide
- Guide_Snap_Right_End_delete_or_hide
- Guide_Collider_LowWall_Box_delete_or_hide
- Guide_Dog_Blocking_Volume_delete_or_hide
- Guide_Interaction_Point_SniffFence

Unity setup:
1. Import OBJ and MTL together.
2. Create a prefab under something like:
   Assets/A_Dogs_Tale/Resources/Prefabs/Terrain/Fences/
3. Delete or hide Guide_* groups before final visual use, or keep them on an editor/helper layer.
4. Suggested colliders:
   - One BoxCollider using Guide_Collider_LowWall_Box_delete_or_hide dimensions.
   - If dogs can peek through or small animals pass through, use separate post/rail colliders instead.
5. Snap panels using:
   - Guide_Snap_Left_End_delete_or_hide
   - Guide_Snap_Right_End_delete_or_hide
   These are placed at x = -1.5 and x = +1.5.

Gameplay ideas:
- Low wall that blocks dogs but lets cats/squirrels jump over.
- Dogs can sniff along it, bark through it, or follow scent trails to a gate.
- Could separate yard zones without feeling like a dungeon wall.
- Useful as a visibility/line-of-sight partial blocker.
- Can be paired with a matching gate prefab later.

OBJ limitation:
- OBJ stores mesh/materials only.
- It does not store Unity colliders, snap sockets, scripts, prefab variants, nav blockers, or gate behavior.

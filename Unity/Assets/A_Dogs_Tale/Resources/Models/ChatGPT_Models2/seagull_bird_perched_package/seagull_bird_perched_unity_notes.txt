Perched Seagull Bird OBJ - Unity Notes

Files:
- seagull_bird_perched.obj
- seagull_bird_perched.mtl

Design:
- Static perched seagull with folded wings.
- White body/head, grey wings, black wingtips, yellow beak with red spot.
- Webbed pink feet gripping a simple perch/rail.
- Includes subtle plumage marks, tail feathers, eye highlights, and optional squawk arcs.
- Guide markers included for perch placement, dog approach, look target, collider, and flee trigger.

Important named groups:
- Simple_round_perch_branch_or_rail
- White_oval_seagull_body
- White_chest_belly_round
- Short_white_neck
- Round_white_seagull_head
- Left_folded_grey_wing
- Right_folded_grey_wing
- *_black_wingtip_patch
- Short_fanned_tail_feathers
- Long_yellow_seagull_beak_upper
- Lower_yellow_beak_small
- Red_spot_on_lower_beak
- Left_black_seagull_eye / Right_black_seagull_eye
- Left_webbed_foot_gripping_perch
- Right_webbed_foot_gripping_perch
- Optional_squawk_sound_arc_*
- Guide_Collider_Seagull_Body_delete_or_hide
- Guide_Bird_Perch_Point
- Guide_Bird_Look_Target
- Guide_Dog_Sniff_Approach_Point
- Guide_Alert_Flee_Trigger_delete_or_hide

Unity setup:
1. Import OBJ and MTL together.
2. Create a prefab under something like:
   Assets/A_Dogs_Tale/Resources/Prefabs/Animals/Birds/
3. Delete or hide Guide_* groups before final visual use, or keep them on an editor/helper layer.
4. Suggested colliders:
   - Small CapsuleCollider around body/head.
   - Optional trigger around Guide_Alert_Flee_Trigger_delete_or_hide.
   - Optional small trigger at Guide_Bird_Perch_Point for perch assignment.
5. If placing on an existing fence/tree/birdbath, hide or delete Simple_round_perch_branch_or_rail.

Gameplay ideas for A Dog's Tale:
- Bird perches on fence, birdbath, roof, branch, or trash can.
- Dog approaches; seagull squawks and flies away.
- Seagull can steal food/kibble or guard a nest.
- Can react to dog bark, squeaky toy, sprinkler water, or Roomba noise.
- Useful behaviors:
  - BirdPerch
  - BirdLookAtDog
  - BirdAlarmCall
  - BirdFlee
  - ReactToSound
  - ReactToDogApproach

OBJ limitation:
- OBJ stores mesh/materials only.
- It does not store Unity colliders, triggers, scripts, bird AI, rigging, animations, flight paths, or state transitions.

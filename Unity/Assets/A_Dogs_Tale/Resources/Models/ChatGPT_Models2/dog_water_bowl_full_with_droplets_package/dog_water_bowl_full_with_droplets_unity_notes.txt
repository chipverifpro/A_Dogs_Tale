Dog Water Bowl Full with Spilled Droplets - Unity Notes

Files:
- dog_water_bowl_full_with_droplets.obj
- dog_water_bowl_full_with_droplets.mtl

Design:
- Same red plastic dog bowl style as the kibble version.
- Filled with a slightly rippled transparent water surface.
- Water highlight/ripple rings on the bowl surface.
- Small spilled puddle and individual droplets in front of the bowl.
- Small bone-shaped label on the front.
- Guide markers for collider, water source, drinking interaction, and spill target placement.

Important named groups:
- Dog_water_bowl_outer_red_plastic_shell
- Dog_water_bowl_inner_dark_red_wall
- Dog_water_bowl_thick_rounded_top_rim
- Dog_water_bowl_inner_bottom_disk
- Dog_water_bowl_bottom_foot_ring
- Transparent_water_surface_slightly_rippled
- Water_surface_ripple_ring_center
- Water_surface_ripple_ring_wide
- Water_surface_small_drink_disturbance
- Small_spilled_water_puddle_front
- Spilled_water_droplet_*
- Bowl_front_label_*
- Guide_Collider_Bowl_CylinderApprox_delete_or_hide
- Guide_Water_Source_delete_or_hide
- Guide_Interaction_Point_DrinkOrSniff
- Guide_Spilled_Water_Target

Unity setup:
1. Import OBJ and MTL together.
2. Create a prefab under something like:
   Assets/A_Dogs_Tale/Resources/Prefabs/Props/Dog/
3. Delete or hide Guide_* groups before final visual use, or keep them on an editor/helper layer.
4. The water materials include MTL transparency, but Unity may import them as opaque.
   If so:
   - Set water materials to Transparent.
   - Use alpha around 0.35-0.55.
   - Increase Smoothness/Specular for a wet look.
5. Suggested colliders:
   - Cylinder-like collider or short CapsuleCollider for the bowl.
   - Optional trigger over Guide_Water_Source_delete_or_hide.
   - Optional trigger over Guide_Water_Splash_Area_delete_or_hide.

Gameplay ideas:
- Dog can drink, sniff, splash, knock over, or track wet pawprints after stepping in it.
- Roomba can drag water into a short wet trail.
- Can act as a hydration/rest behavior target.
- Dogs could react differently if the bowl is full, empty, dirty, or spilled.

OBJ limitation:
- OBJ stores mesh/materials only.
- It does not store Unity colliders, triggers, scripts, water shaders, particle effects, or drink behavior.

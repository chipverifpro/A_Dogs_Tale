Garden Hose Between Water Tap and Sprinkler - Unity Notes

Files:
- garden_hose_between_tap_and_sprinkler.obj
- garden_hose_between_tap_and_sprinkler.mtl

Design:
- Flexible green garden hose laid on the ground.
- Intended to visually connect the outdoor water tap to the rotating sprinkler.
- Brass connector/coupler on both ends.
- Ribbed connector rings, black strain-relief collars, subtle scuffs, and a tiny leak/drop near the tap end.
- Guide markers included for tap attachment, sprinkler attachment, hose collider, trip hazard trigger, and interaction point.

Approximate placement:
- Tap end starts around x = -0.22, y = 0.12, z = 0.02.
- Sprinkler end finishes around x = 3.24, y = 0.105, z = -0.37.
- Designed as a simple curved connector segment. You can scale/rotate it to fit your specific tap and sprinkler positions.

Unity setup:
1. Import OBJ and MTL together.
2. Create a prefab under something like:
   Assets/A_Dogs_Tale/Resources/Prefabs/Props/Garden/
3. Delete or hide Guide_* groups before final visual use, or keep them on an editor/helper layer.
4. Suggested colliders:
   - Use a few CapsuleColliders or thin BoxColliders along the hose path.
   - Use Guide_Trip_Hazard_Trigger_delete_or_hide as a trigger for dogs tripping, noticing, or stepping over.
   - Use Guide_Tap_Attach_Point and Guide_Sprinkler_Attach_Point as snap/reference points.
5. If the water/leak imports opaque:
   - Set water materials to Transparent.
   - Use alpha around 0.35-0.55.

Gameplay ideas for A Dog's Tale:
- Hose connects tap state to sprinkler state.
- Dog can sniff, drag, chew, trip over, or follow the hose.
- Roomba can bump into it or get tangled.
- If hose is disconnected, sprinkler should not work.
- Hose could create a scent/visual trail between tap and sprinkler.
- Tiny leak can create wet pawprints or dilute scent locally.

OBJ limitation:
- OBJ stores mesh/materials only.
- It does not store Unity colliders, snap sockets, scripts, hose physics, water simulation, connection state, or sprinkler/tap logic.

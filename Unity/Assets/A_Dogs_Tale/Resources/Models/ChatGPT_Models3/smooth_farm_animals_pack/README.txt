Smooth Farm Animals Model Pack
==============================

Included:
- smooth_sheep.obj
- smooth_dairy_cow.obj
- smooth_brown_bull.obj
- smooth_farm_animals.mtl
- manifest.json

These are smoother replacements for the earlier blocky farm animals. They are still
low-poly and game-friendly, but use ellipsoid bodies, rounded heads, tapered legs,
larger curved-looking horns, organic cow patches, and softer silhouettes.

Suggested Unity folder:
Assets/A_Dogs_Tale/Resources/Models/Animals/Farm/Smooth/

Suggested prefab folder:
Assets/A_Dogs_Tale/Resources/Prefabs/Animals/Farm/

General notes:
- Forward direction is +X.
- Scale is approximate Unity meters.
- Use simple colliders: body CapsuleCollider/BoxCollider plus optional head collider.
- The OBJ parts are named so you can split/recolor pieces if desired.
- For clean transforms, create an empty prefab root and put the imported OBJ under ModelRoot.

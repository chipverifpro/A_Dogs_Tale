Natural Quadruped Squirrel OBJ - Rigging / Unity Notes

Contents:
- squirrel_natural_quadruped_pose.obj
- squirrel_natural_quadruped_pose.mtl

Changes from the upright stylized squirrel:
- Body is now horizontal and longer.
- Head is forward and slightly lowered.
- All four paws are placed on the ground plane at y=0.
- Hind legs are crouched under the hips.
- Front legs are under the shoulders.
- Tail is arched over the back rather than standing straight upright.
- Removed the acorn prop to keep this version focused on a natural walking/standing pose.

Coordinate notes:
- The squirrel faces negative Z.
- Ground plane is approximately y=0.
- Pivot/import origin is near the body center.

Suggested armature:
Root/Hips
  SpineMid
    Chest
      Neck
        Head
          LeftEar
          RightEar
      LeftFrontShoulder -> LeftFrontElbow -> LeftFrontPaw
      RightFrontShoulder -> RightFrontElbow -> RightFrontPaw
  LeftHip -> LeftKnee -> LeftHindPaw
  RightHip -> RightKnee -> RightHindPaw
  TailBase -> TailMid01 -> TailMid02 -> TailTip

Animation ideas:
- Natural quadruped walk/hop cycle
- Alert pause with tail twitch
- Sniffing the ground
- Quick skitter/run loop
- Sitting-up transition from this pose to the previous upright pose

OBJ limitation:
- OBJ stores mesh groups and materials, not bones or animation clips.
- Add armature and weights in Blender, then export FBX for Unity animation.

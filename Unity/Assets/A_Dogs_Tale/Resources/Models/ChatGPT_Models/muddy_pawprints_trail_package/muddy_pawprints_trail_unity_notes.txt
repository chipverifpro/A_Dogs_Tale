Muddy Pawprints Trail OBJ - Unity Notes

Files:
- muddy_pawprints_trail.obj
- muddy_pawprints_trail.mtl

Design:
- A short alternating left/right trail of muddy dog pawprints.
- Each print includes:
  - Main pad
  - Four toe pads
  - Small claw marks
  - Mud smears
  - Random splatter dots
- The geometry is very thin and sits close to the floor like a decal mesh.
- Includes start/end/area guide markers for scent tracking gameplay.

Important named groups:
- Pawprint_01_left_main_pad_center
- Pawprint_01_left_toe_pad_*
- Pawprint_01_left_small_claw_mark_*
- Pawprint_01_left_mud_smear_heel_drag
- Pawprint_*_mud_splatter_*
- Partial_muddy_step_start_*
- Guide_Track_Start_delete_or_hide
- Guide_Track_End_delete_or_hide
- Guide_Scent_Trail_Area_delete_or_hide

Unity setup:
1. Import OBJ and MTL together.
2. Create a prefab under something like:
   Assets/A_Dogs_Tale/Resources/Prefabs/Clues/Tracks/
3. Place slightly above the floor to avoid z-fighting.
   Suggested y offset: 0.005 to 0.015 above the floor.
4. Delete or hide Guide_* groups before final use, or keep them on an editor/helper layer.
5. If the translucent mud smear imports opaque, set the material Surface Type to Transparent and alpha around 0.5-0.7.

Gameplay ideas:
- Use as a visible clue plus scent trail.
- Guide_Track_Start can mark where the scent is strongest/oldest.
- Guide_Track_End can point toward the next clue.
- Dogs can sniff the trail and follow it.
- Rain, Roomba, or foot traffic could weaken or smear the trail.
- ReactToScent or ReactToScentSourceFound can trigger when the dog reaches a print.

OBJ limitation:
- OBJ stores mesh/materials only.
- It does not store Unity decals, colliders, scent emitters, trigger zones, scripts, or particle effects.

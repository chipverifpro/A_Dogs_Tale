Colorful Parrot on Perch Stand - OBJ package

This is a stylized, game-friendly parrot model posed on a perch stand.

Rigging notes:
- OBJ does not store bones, armatures, animation curves, or skin weights.
- Mesh groups are named by body part to simplify selection in Blender or Unity.
- RigGuide_* cubes mark useful bone/pivot locations. Delete or hide them after rigging.
- Suggested bones: root, spine, chest, neck, head, beak, tail, left/right wing root, left/right wing feather controls, left/right leg, left/right foot.
- For idle animation: bob body/head slightly, blink eyes with separate eye material or alternate mesh, twitch tail, flap wings gently.
- For Unity: import the OBJ with its MTL in the same folder. Scale as needed; the perch base sits near y=0.

Major groups:
- Body_rounded_green_chest
- Belly_yellow_front_patch
- Neck_short_red_collar
- Head_round_red
- Head_top_blue_cap
- Left/Right wing groups and feather strips
- Tail_long_colorful_feather_*
- Left/Right leg, foot, and claws
- Perch_horizontal_wooden_rod
- Stand_vertical_metal_pole
- Stand_round_weighted_base

Open-Interior Peaked-Roof Doghouse OBJ - Unity Notes

Files:
- open_interior_peaked_roof_doghouse.obj
- open_interior_peaked_roof_doghouse.mtl

What changed from the first doghouse:
- This version is hollow.
- The large arched doorway is actually open.
- The inside has a walkable floor, interior side wall faces, interior back wall, and roof underfaces.
- Collider guide pieces are split around the doorway so they do not block the dog.
- Guide_Interior_Walkable_Volume_clear_space shows the empty volume intended for the dog character.

Unity collider setup:
1. Delete or hide all Guide_* objects before final visual use, or keep them on an EditorOnly/helper layer.
2. Add colliders manually rather than one solid BoxCollider:
   - Left wall BoxCollider
   - Right wall BoxCollider
   - Back wall BoxCollider
   - Front-left wall BoxCollider
   - Front-right wall BoxCollider
   - Front header BoxCollider above door
   - Optional roof colliders
3. Do NOT add a single solid collider around the whole doghouse, or it will block the entry.
4. Add a trigger at Guide_Door_Entry_Point for “enter doghouse”.
5. Add a target/rest transform at Guide_Dog_Rest_Point_Inside.

Suggested gameplay:
- Doorway trigger can switch the dog to an EnterDoghouse task.
- Inside rest point can be a sit/lie-down/idle target.
- Doghouse can provide shelter, hiding, sleep, or safe-zone behavior.
- Roof can optionally be walkable/perchable if you add roof colliders.

OBJ limitation:
- OBJ stores mesh/materials only.
- It does not store colliders, triggers, scripts, transforms, prefabs, or animations.

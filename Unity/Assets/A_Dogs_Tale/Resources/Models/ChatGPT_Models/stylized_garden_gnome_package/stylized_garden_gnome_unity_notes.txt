Stylized Garden Gnome OBJ - Unity Notes

Files:
- stylized_garden_gnome.obj
- stylized_garden_gnome.mtl

Design:
- Cartoon garden gnome prop.
- Red pointed hat, blue tunic, white beard, large round nose, black boots.
- Includes small shovel, stone base, moss spots, and dirt smudge.
- Good as a suspicious dog-world object: bark-at target, scent clue, obstacle, or yard decoration.

Important named groups:
- Round_grass_stone_base
- Blue_tunic_body
- Black_belt_band
- Gold_square_belt_buckle
- Peach_round_face
- Large_round_gnome_nose
- White_beard_main_fluffy_triangle
- White_beard_lump_*
- Red_hat_brim
- Tall_red_pointed_hat
- Left_blue_sleeve_arm / Right_blue_sleeve_arm
- Tiny_gnome_shovel_*
- Tiny_moss_spot_*
- Guide_Collider_Gnome_Box_delete_or_hide
- Guide_Interaction_Point_BarkAtGnome
- Guide_Scent_Source_MysteriousGardenSmell

Unity setup:
1. Import OBJ and MTL together.
2. Create a prefab under something like:
   Assets/A_Dogs_Tale/Resources/Prefabs/Props/Garden/
3. Delete or hide Guide_* groups before final use, or keep them on an editor/helper layer.
4. Suggested colliders:
   - BoxCollider or CapsuleCollider around the whole gnome.
   - Optional small trigger near Guide_Interaction_Point_BarkAtGnome.
   - Optional scent trigger near Guide_Scent_Source_MysteriousGardenSmell.

Gameplay ideas:
- Dogs may bark at it because it looks like a tiny suspicious person.
- It can hold a clue scent: squirrel, mud, food, garden chemical, or unknown visitor.
- Squirrels can perch on it or use it as cover.
- Roomba can bump it and knock it over.
- Could be part of a puzzle: move gnome, reveal hidden key/bone, or mark buried object.

OBJ limitation:
- OBJ stores mesh/materials only.
- It does not store Unity colliders, triggers, scripts, scent emitters, breakable behavior, or animations.

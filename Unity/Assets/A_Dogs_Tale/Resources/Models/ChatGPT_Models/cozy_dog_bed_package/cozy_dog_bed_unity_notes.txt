Cozy Dog Bed OBJ - Unity Notes

Files:
- cozy_dog_bed.obj
- cozy_dog_bed.mtl

Design:
- Soft oval dog bed.
- Raised outer bolster/rim.
- Lower front entry lip so the dog can plausibly step in.
- Depressed center cushion for a curled-up resting pose.
- Fabric wrinkles, seam piping, small sewn tag, and paw embroidery.
- Guide objects included for colliders/rest/scent placement.

Important named groups:
- Soft_floor_contact_shadow
- Depressed_soft_center_cushion
- Raised_oval_outer_bolster_pillow_rim
- Lower_front_entry_lip_soft_pad
- Inner_cushion_seam_piping_*
- Outer_bolster_lower_seam_piping_*
- Subtle_fabric_wrinkle_center_*
- Small_fabric_tag_on_front
- Embroidered_paw_print_on_cushion_*
- Guide_Collider_Bed_Box_delete_or_hide
- Guide_Rest_Target_Dog_Curl_Up_delete_or_hide
- Guide_Comfort_Scent_Area_delete_or_hide

Unity setup:
1. Import OBJ and MTL together.
2. Create a prefab under something like:
   Assets/A_Dogs_Tale/Resources/Prefabs/Props/Dog/
3. Delete or hide Guide_* objects before final visual use, or keep them on an editor/helper layer.
4. Suggested colliders:
   - One BoxCollider or low CapsuleCollider for the bed base.
   - Optional trigger over Guide_Rest_Target_Dog_Curl_Up_delete_or_hide.
   - Optional scent/comfort trigger over Guide_Comfort_Scent_Area_delete_or_hide.
5. The shadow and guide materials include transparency in the MTL, but Unity may import them as opaque.
   If so, set them to Transparent or delete/hide them.

Gameplay ideas:
- Rest/sleep/idle target.
- Comfort scent source for the dog.
- Pack gathering point.
- Safe zone or healing/stamina recovery object.
- Dogs can carry toys/socks/bones back to the bed.
- Good target for Stay, Rest, CurlUp, Guard, or Dream behaviors.

OBJ limitation:
- OBJ stores mesh/materials only.
- It does not store Unity colliders, triggers, scripts, animations, prefabs, or behavior logic.

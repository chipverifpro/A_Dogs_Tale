Roomba-style Robot Vacuum OBJ Notes
===================================

This is a generic, game-ready robot vacuum inspired by common round household robot vacuums.
It intentionally avoids exact brand logos or proprietary markings.

OBJ contents:
- Separate named groups for top/body/bumper/wheels/brushes/buttons/sensors.
- Small RigGuide_* cubes mark useful animation pivots. Delete them after rig setup if desired.

Suggested Unity scale:
- Import at scale 1.0, then adjust to match your tile/world scale. The mesh is about 2.5 units wide.
- For a Unity floor tile of 1x1, you may want import scale around 0.25 to 0.35.

Suggested rig/animation pivots:
- Root: center of disk.
- Drive wheels: rotate Left_drive_wheel_visible_rubber and Right_drive_wheel_visible_rubber around local X or Y depending on Blender orientation after import.
- Side brush: rotate Side_spinning_brush_* around RigGuide_side_brush_pivot.
- Bumper: make Front_soft_collision_bumper_arc a child of a bumper bone; animate a tiny backward bump on contact.

Dog-opponent behavior ideas:
- Patrol in slow arcs.
- Emit low motor hum.
- Trigger dog curiosity/fear/bark reactions when it moves, bumps, or spins its brush.
- Add a collider capsule/cylinder and a separate front trigger collider for bumper contact.

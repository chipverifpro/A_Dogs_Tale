Bare Squirrel Climbing Tree OBJ - Unity Notes

Files:
- bare_squirrel_climbing_tree.obj
- bare_squirrel_climbing_tree.mtl

Design:
- Bare deciduous-style tree with a broad trunk and wide-reaching climbable branches.
- Intended for squirrels to run, jump, hide, perch, or throw acorns from.
- No leaves, so it stays visually readable for gameplay and pathfinding.
- Branches are named individually for easy selection in Blender/Unity.

Important named groups:
- Trunk_main_broad_slightly_twisted
- Root_*
- Branch_A_left_low_wide_climbable
- Branch_B_right_low_wide_climbable
- Branch_C_back_sweeping_runway
- Branch_D_front_sweeping_runway
- Branch_E_upper_left_reaching
- Branch_F_upper_right_reaching
- Fork_*
- Knot_*
- Bark_vertical_trunk_ridge_*
- PathGuide_* markers

Unity use:
1. Import OBJ and MTL into something like:
   Assets/A_Dogs_Tale/Resources/Prefabs/Terrain/Trees/
2. Create a prefab from the imported model.
3. Delete or hide the PathGuide_* marker cubes before final visual use, or keep them on an editor-only/helper layer.
4. Add colliders manually:
   - CapsuleCollider along the trunk.
   - CapsuleCollider or custom primitive colliders along major branches.
   - Avoid MeshCollider unless you need exact collisions; primitive colliders are cheaper and better for AI movement.
5. For squirrel navigation:
   - Treat branches as graph edges.
   - Use PathGuide_* markers as suggested node positions.
   - Add additional empty GameObjects along branches for finer squirrel path points.
6. For dog gameplay:
   - Make the lower trunk sniffable.
   - Put squirrel interaction/perch points on Branch_A and Branch_B.
   - Give dogs a bark-at-tree or investigate-tree interaction.
7. For acorn throwing:
   - Good throw/perch points are the ends of Branch_A, Branch_B, Branch_C, and Branch_D.
   - Emit a Noise event when the acorn hits the ground or a Roomba/dog prop.

Suggested scale:
- If 1 Unity unit equals roughly 1 meter, this imports as about 5.1 units tall.
- For an indoor toy/tree prop, scale down.
- For an outdoor yard tree, keep as-is or scale to 1.2-1.6.

OBJ limitations:
- OBJ stores geometry and materials only.
- It does not store Unity colliders, path graphs, animations, or prefabs.

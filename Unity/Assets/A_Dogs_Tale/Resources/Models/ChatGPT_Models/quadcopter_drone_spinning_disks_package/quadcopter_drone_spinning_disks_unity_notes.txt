Quadcopter Drone with Solid Propeller Disks - Unity Notes

Files:
- quadcopter_drone_spinning_disks.obj
- quadcopter_drone_spinning_disks.mtl

Design:
- Small quadcopter drone suitable as a moving hazard, scout, toy, or dog opponent.
- Solid propeller blur disks are used instead of separate propeller blades.
- This lets the drone look like the props are spinning even when the mesh is static.
- Includes prop guard rings, landing skids, camera gimbal, body shell, antenna, and navigation LEDs.

Unity import tips:
1. Import OBJ and MTL together.
2. Create a prefab under something like:
   Assets/A_Dogs_Tale/Resources/Prefabs/Props/Drones/
3. Delete or hide RigGuide_* objects for final visual use.
4. The propeller_blur_disk material includes MTL transparency fields:
   d 0.38 / Tr 0.62
   Unity may not automatically make the material transparent. If needed:
   - Set the material Surface Type to Transparent.
   - Reduce alpha to around 0.35-0.45.
   - Use alpha blending, not cutout.
5. Add a Rigidbody only if physics-driven. Otherwise move it with your AI/controller.

Suggested colliders:
- SphereCollider or BoxCollider around central body.
- Optional small trigger sphere around propeller disk area for “danger/noise zone.”
- Avoid MeshCollider for the prop disks unless needed.

Gameplay ideas for A Dog's Tale:
- Emits a high-pitched Noise event while flying.
- Dogs can bark at it, chase it, flee from it, or hide under furniture.
- Squirrels can throw acorns at it.
- The camera gimbal can act as a Vision sensor.
- It can patrol rooms, scan scent trails, or act as a comic airborne rival.

Animation ideas:
- You do not need spinning prop animation because of the solid blur disks.
- Good simple animations:
  - Body bobbing up/down.
  - Slight roll/pitch while turning.
  - Camera gimbal looking around.
  - LED blinking.
  - Gentle prop disk rotation if desired, but optional.

OBJ limitation:
- OBJ stores mesh and materials only.
- It does not store Unity scripts, colliders, transparency shader settings, Rigidbody, animation clips, lights, or particle effects.

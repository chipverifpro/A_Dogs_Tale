Single Dropped Sock OBJ - Unity Notes

Files:
- single_dropped_sock.obj
- single_dropped_sock.mtl

Design:
- A single soft, crumpled dropped sock.
- White/off-white fabric with grey cuff, heel patch, toe seam, and wrinkles.
- Open dark cuff end and closed rounded toe.
- Optional green scent wisps included as separate named groups.
- Guide objects included for collider and scent source placement.

Important named groups:
- Sock_soft_body_tube_with_closed_toe
- Sock_dark_hollow_opening_inside_cuff
- Sock_cuff_flattened_top_band
- Sock_cuff_flattened_bottom_band
- Sock_cuff_rib_line_*
- Sock_soft_fold_wrinkle_*
- Sock_toe_curved_seam
- Sock_subtle_heel_patch_light_grey
- Optional_sock_scent_wisp_*
- Guide_Collider_Sock_Box_delete_or_hide
- Guide_Scent_Source_Area_delete_or_hide

Unity setup:
1. Import OBJ and MTL together.
2. Create a prefab under something like:
   Assets/A_Dogs_Tale/Resources/Prefabs/Props/Clothing/
3. Delete or hide Guide_* groups before final visual use, or keep them on an editor/helper layer.
4. Suggested collider:
   - Small BoxCollider or CapsuleCollider around the sock.
   - Trigger collider over Guide_Scent_Source_Area_delete_or_hide for scent detection.
5. If using the scent wisps as real visuals, keep Optional_sock_scent_wisp_*.
   Otherwise delete/hide them and let your scent system visualize scent separately.

Gameplay ideas:
- Strong owner scent clue.
- Dog can sniff, carry, bury, retrieve, or present it proudly.
- Could identify a room/person or act as a trail start object.
- Excellent target for ReactToScentSourceFound.

OBJ limitation:
- OBJ stores mesh/materials only.
- It does not store Unity colliders, triggers, scripts, scent emitter components, or pickup behavior.
